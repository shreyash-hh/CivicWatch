About the Movement

Everyday actions build the India we share.

Civic Sense Heroes is CivicWatch's nationwide platform to recognise citizens who quietly improve the places and systems we all share. We find these unsung heroes, celebrate their verified work with photographic proof, and inspire millions more Indians to see civic responsibility as a daily expression of patriotism.

How the Movement Works

Find · Celebrate · Inspire

A three-step revolution for civic consciousness across India.

01

Find

Citizens improving the places and systems we all share. Spot infrastructure hazards with GPS-located photos, or discover volunteers and NGOs already working in your locality through our verified registry.

02

Celebrate

The people whose actions make communities cleaner, safer, more accessible and more responsible. Side-by-side Before/After evidence, verified civic credits, and tamper-proof digital certificates.

03

Inspire

More Indians to see civic responsibility as a daily expression of patriotism. When one citizen takes responsibility, an entire neighbourhood sees what's possible — and acts.
