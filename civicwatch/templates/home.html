How CivicWatch Works

From Hazard to Verified Transformation

A trusted public infrastructure cycle ensuring no grievance goes unanswered.

01

1. Spot & Report

Citizens document infrastructure defects with GPS-located photos. EXIF data is sanitized for privacy, and geo-radius duplicate detection prevents redundancy.

02

2. Adopt & Remediate

Verified volunteers, NGO teams, and civic workers adopt open incidents from the live map. Anti-self-dealing rules guarantee objective, unconflicted execution.

03

3. Verify & Celebrate

Side-by-side Before/After evidence is reviewed. Citizens rate the fix (1-5 stars), resolvers earn civic credits, and tamper-proof digital certificates are issued.
